How this maps to BenchDouble

  1. The DI container injects a ResistiveLoadBehavior or TimedOvercurrentBehavior into the mock power supply.
  2. A run-scoped simulation clock advances the load and produces UnitsNet current/voltage samples.
  3. The monitor evaluates the overcurrent rule and records a timestamped event.
  4. A future INotificationSink would send through configured email/SMS providers, with delivery status, retries and authorization; this demo uses only an in-browser simulated sink.
  5. In a production system, safety shutdown must be independent of email delivery and must verify physical instrument state.
